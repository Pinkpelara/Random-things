# Leap Gymnastics voice guidelines

Generated 20 November 2026 from Leap's website copy (home, about, programs, camps, birthday parties, FAQ, Try Leap, Leap Updates; saved 23 October 2026) and the owner's working rules. Purpose: the staff and family app.

## We are / we are not

| We are | We are not | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Clear: everyday words, the point first | Clever, jokey or full of jargon | High |
| Calm and reassuring | Hyped. No exclamation marks in system text. | High |
| Exact about rules, dates and money | Vague, or promising things Leap hasn't published | High |
| Warm and respectful to a teen coach and a parent alike | Cute, childish or talking down | Medium: the site speaks to parents; there is no published writing for staff |

## Tone by context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Routine staff notice | Brief, neutral | "Saturday's 3:30 party needs two hosts." |
| Urgent or safety | Plain, action first, no emoji | "Gym closed today. All classes are cancelled." |
| Training | Direct, one idea per line | "The floor holds 40 people at one time, counting parents." |
| Update for families | Slightly more formal, complete sentences | "PA Day camp runs 8:30 am to 5:30 pm. Pack a labelled water bottle, lunch and two snacks." |
| Policy | Exact, in Leap's published words | "Withdrawal requires 15 days' written notice." |
| Error | What happened, then what to do | "Message not sent. Check your connection and try again." |

## Mechanics
- Sentence case. Buttons are verbs that say what happens.
- Canadian spelling: enrolment, labelled, cancelled.
- Dates as "Friday, November 20". Times as "4:30 pm". Digits for numbers.
- Prices that aren't tax-inclusive say "plus HST".
- Say who can see something wherever it matters.

## Terms
- gymnast or athlete, not "kid"
- family, parent or guardian
- coach, class, program
- make-up class, Try Leap drop-in, PA Day camp
- Program names exactly as published: Leap Together Parent & Tot, Little Leapers, Ready Set Leap, Rising Stars, Flight Crew, Boys Adventure Gym, Floor Tumbling & Obstacle Skills, Leap Plus, Leap Elite Foundations

## Never
- Invent a policy, price, date or FAQ.
- Reword legal or consent text.
- Use humour or emoji in urgent, safety or family messages.

## Open questions
1. "Leap" or "LEAP"? The site uses both. Recommended: "Leap" in running text.
2. Time format. The site writes "4:00pm" on class listings and "8:30 a.m." on the camps page. Recommended for the app: "4:30 pm", following the Canada.ca style guide.
3. Is there any existing writing addressed to staff? None was found, so the staff tone above is inferred.
